# LinkedIn Post

B2B teams do not have a lead-volume problem. They have an intelligence problem.

A list of company names is not enough. Before a sales conversation starts, a team needs to know whether the company is real, how to reach it, what evidence supports the record, whether the business fits the target profile, and where there may be a practical CRM or automation opportunity.

This platform turns that research process into a structured lead-intelligence workflow. Discovery can pull from compliant sources such as Google Places and Exa, followed by website enrichment, normalization, deduplication, verification, and two separate scores: a CRM Opportunity Score and a Data Quality Score. That separation matters — a strong commercial opportunity should not be confused with a fully verified record.

The output is designed for action rather than just storage. Qualified records are organized into a canonical lead database, evidence and source URLs are preserved, cold-call briefs and recommended openings can be generated from verified information, and the pipeline produces structured Excel exports for sales workflows.

The interface brings the workflow into one professional Streamlit workspace for discovery, lead review, verification, scoring, cold-call preparation, exports, connector health, and configuration status. xAI/Grok can be used for the final language-polishing step without becoming the source of business facts.

The core pipeline is intentionally built around traceability: when a fact is not publicly verified, it stays unverified instead of being filled with a guess.
